# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small, focused project built by and for people trying to get a specific piece of hardware working. Everyone who shows up here — whether you're reporting a bug, submitting a fix, or just asking a question — deserves to be treated with patience and respect.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, hardware setup, or how long you've been staring at `SPI returns 0x00`.

## Our standards

**Behaviour that makes this project better:**

- Being patient with people who are new to LoRaWAN, Linux, or embedded hardware
- Sharing what you've tried and what didn't work — that information helps everyone
- Giving and receiving feedback constructively
- Acknowledging when someone else's debugging saved you hours

**Behaviour that's not welcome:**

- Dismissing questions because the answer seems obvious to you
- Posting unhelpful comments like "just use a Raspberry Pi" on issues for unsupported hardware
- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind
- Sharing others' private information without permission

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit comments.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by opening a private discussion or contacting the maintainer directly via GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and responded to. The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban users who violate this code of conduct.
